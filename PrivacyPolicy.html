This Privacy Policy describes the procedures for the processing of personal data carried out through the G-Scanner mobile and web application, developed in compliance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and the applicable Italian legislation on personal data protection.

Purpose of the Application

G-Scanner is an application that allows users to consult information related to food products by scanning barcodes, with particular attention to the needs of people suffering from celiac disease or lactose intolerance.

The application also allows users to configure specific dietary preferences, receive indications based on pre-set informational filters, and participate in a community by sharing product reports.

Important – Limitation of Liability

The information provided by G-Scanner is exclusively for informational and user support purposes.

The application does not constitute a medical device, does not provide medical advice, has no diagnostic or therapeutic value, and does not produce legally binding effects or evaluations.

The displayed information does not in any way replace:

  • the advice of a doctor or other qualified healthcare professional;
  • the consultation of the official food product labels;
  • the information provided directly by the manufacturer.

The evaluations generated by the application are based exclusively on the data available in the database and the settings configured by the user, and may not reflect variations in product composition, recipe updates by manufacturers, or unavailable information.

The user is always required to independently verify the composition and labeling of products prior to consumption.

2. Types of Data Processed

The application processes exclusively the data necessary for its operation and the provision of the offered features.

2.1 Anonymous users

When G-Scanner is used without logging in, the user's personal data and configured preferences remain exclusively on the device and are stored locally via SharedPreferences.

These include:

  • scan history;
  • application settings;
  • preferences related to dietary and health features selected by the user.

Such personal data is not transmitted to the Data Controller's servers.

It is understood, however, that product reports, if submitted through the application, are stored in the application's cloud database and made available to other users of the community in order to improve the service.

Reports published in the community may exclusively contain:

  • information relating to the reported food or product;
  • any notes entered by the user;
  • the reason for the report.

The identity of the reporting user, including first name, last name, email address, or other identifying data, is never made public or visibly associated with the report under any circumstances.

Reports made by other users can also be viewed by application users, regardless of authentication.

2.2 Authenticated users

The user may authenticate through Firebase Authentication using an account from:

  • Google;
  • Facebook.

Following authentication, the data necessary to identify the user is acquired from the provider, which may include:

  • first name;
  • last name;
  • email address;
  • phone number, where available or associated with the profile used for authentication.

A unique user identifier (User ID) is associated with this information.

G-Scanner does not access the user's authentication credentials, such as passwords or equivalent tools, and does not process such information.

2.3 Data stored in the cloud database

For authenticated users, the following data is stored on Firebase Firestore, associated with the user's identifier:

  • scan history;
  • list of submitted reports;
  • settings related to dietary preferences:
    • Additives Warning;
    • Strict Contamination Filter;
    • Lactose Intolerance;
    • any settings related to contamination warnings;
  • selected language;
  • chosen graphical theme.

The identifying data obtained through authentication (first name, last name, email address, and any available phone number) is used exclusively to:

  • allow account management;
  • correctly associate user data with their profile;
  • provide features reserved for authenticated users.

2.4 Cookies and Local Storage Technologies

G-Scanner (both in the Web App version and the Mobile application) does not use profiling cookies, advertising tracking tools, or third-party analytics systems.

The application exclusively uses strictly necessary local storage technologies (such as SharedPreferences on mobile devices and LocalStorage / Technical cookies on browsers) for the sole purpose of:

  • Safely maintaining the active user session via Firebase Authentication;
  • Locally storing user preferences (e.g., language, graphical theme) and the confirmation of acceptance of legal documents.

Since these are exclusively technical tools strictly necessary for the provision of the service requested by the user, pursuant to European regulations (ePrivacy Directive) and the rulings of the Italian Data Protection Authority, prior user consent is not required for their use. The user is informed of their presence through this Privacy Policy, without the need for banners or separate documents.

Locally stored data remains on the user's device until deleted or until the application is uninstalled.

3. Special Categories of Personal Data (Art. 9 GDPR)

G-Scanner allows the user to configure specific personal preferences aimed at consulting information on food products. These settings may reflect the user's health status or particular dietary needs and, therefore, may constitute special categories of personal data under Article 9 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

The settings available in the application are as follows:

  • Additives Warning: generates a warning in the presence of ingredients such as modified starches or flavorings whose origin is not specified, allowing the user to perform further checks.
  • Strict Contamination Filter: considers as "Forbidden" any food whose label contains statements such as "may contain traces of gluten" or equivalent wording relating to possible gluten contamination.
  • Lactose Intolerance: checks for the presence of ingredients such as lactose, butter, milk powder, or whey, signaling their potential presence according to the application's functionality.

Upon the first launch of the application, only the following options are enabled by default:

  • Additives Warning;
  • Strict Contamination Filter.

The Lactose Intolerance option is initially disabled and can be enabled by the user at any time.

The processing of this information takes place exclusively subject to the user's explicit consent, pursuant to Article 9, paragraph 2, letter a) of the GDPR.

The user is free to modify, activate, or deactivate the aforementioned settings at any time according to their personal needs.

These choices are made under the user's responsibility, who acknowledges that G-Scanner is exclusively an informational support tool and does not replace:

  • the verification of product labels;
  • the information provided by the manufacturer;
  • the advice of a doctor or other qualified healthcare professional.

Any modification to the settings and the use of the information provided by the application is therefore undertaken at the user's sole risk.

The user may at any time modify their preferences or withdraw previously given consent, without affecting the lawfulness of the processing carried out prior to the withdrawal.

9. Data Retention

9.1 Anonymous Users

When the user uses G-Scanner without authenticating, personal data and configured preferences remain stored exclusively locally on the device via SharedPreferences.

Such data is retained until:

  • the user deletes it via the features available in the application;
  • the application is uninstalled;
  • the device is reset or local data is cleared.

Product reports submitted to the community constitute a separate processing activity and are instead retained in the application's cloud database to allow consultation by other users.

9.2 Authenticated Users

For authenticated users, data is retained using a dual-storage method:

  • locally on the user's device, to enable swift application use;
  • in the Firebase Firestore cloud database, to allow synchronization of information across different devices and maintenance of account-associated features.

Data associated with the account is retained until its deletion according to the procedures described in the following section on data erasure.

10. Right to Erasure and Account Management (Art. 17 GDPR)

The user has two distinct methods for managing the deletion of their data.

10.1 Account Deletion via Internal Application Feature

If the user utilizes the specific internal account deletion feature available in G-Scanner, the associated Firebase Authentication profile is deleted.

This operation entails:

  • the permanent removal of the association between the account and the identifying data used for authentication;
  • the deletion of references to email, first name, last name, and any provider data associated with the profile.

However, the deletion of the authentication profile does not automatically trigger the immediate physical destruction of documents present in Firebase Firestore.

Any data present in the cloud database, such as:

  • scan history;
  • application settings;
  • data associated with the user identifier;

can no longer be linked to the user's identity and becomes technically inaccessible through normal use of the application.

The link between such data and the user's identity is permanently severed.

10.2 Complete and Permanent Data Erasure (Data Wipe)

Should the user require the complete and permanent physical erasure of all records associated with their identifier in cloud systems, they must submit an explicit request to the Data Controller via:

supporto-gscanner@googlegroups.com

The request must be made prior to proceeding with profile deletion via the application, using the same email address associated with the account used for access.

This procedure is necessary so the Data Controller can verify the applicant's identity and correctly locate the records associated with the account.

If the user preemptively deletes their profile from the application without having sent the complete erasure request, it may no longer be possible to verify the applicant's identity and locate the data previously associated with the deleted account.

Following identity verification, the Data Controller will proceed with the permanent erasure of the data present in cloud systems associated with the user's identifier, within the limits technically available and required by applicable law.
