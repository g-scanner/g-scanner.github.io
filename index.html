As simple as a traffic light.

Decoding complex ingredient labels can be exhausting and risky. G-Scanner condenses nutritional data into three universally understood indicators.

Safe

Ingredients are verified safe based on your strict dietary needs (e.g., completely gluten-free).

Uncertain

Potential risks like factory cross-contamination. Always manually double-check the physical label.

Forbidden

Contains gluten or your restricted allergens. Strictly avoid consumption.

SAFE

Gluten-Free Oats

Rating

Celiac friendly

Ingredients

Organic rolled oats

G-Scanner is purely an informational tool and does not replace medical advice. Ratings are generated based on crowdsourced data from the Open Food Facts collaborative database. You are solely responsible for directly verifying the physical labels on products before purchase and consumption.
